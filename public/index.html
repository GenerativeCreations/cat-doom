CATDOOM

The house has been overrun. Only the spray bottle remains.

Move with the pad on the left. Turn and SPRAY with the pad on the right. Every cat you spritz goes down for a nap. Nap them all and the EXIT door opens to the next room. Twelve rooms, each with a new and worse cat, ending with the cat goddess herself. Drink water bowls to refill, eat tuna to heal. From room two on you find new tools: catnip, yarn balls, plastic bags, a cardboard box, a laser pointer, the vacuum, and treats. Number keys 1 to 7 use them. Keyboard also works: WASD or arrow keys (either set), Q/E or Shift+turn to strafe, Space sprays, 1–7 use tools.
